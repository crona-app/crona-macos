# Crona for macOS v1.1.0-beta.8

This beta reorganizes Glance, makes it easier to schedule Away days, and keeps the timer in sync when a Pomodoro break is deferred.

## Glance

- Use an adaptive layout or a single column. Show, hide, and reorder panes within the overview and work area in Settings > Glance, with a layout preview.
- See the Day Summary gauges at a glance. Calendar opens in week view and expands to a month.
- Enable Wellbeing to see check-in status for the selected day and open the check-in form when you need it.
- Select an issue or habit to open its details beside the pointer. Keyboard selection opens details beside the selected row.
- Mark today as away or end manual Away Mode in Glance. Away days have a dedicated screen with date navigation.

## Away settings

- Choose recurring Away days from a row of weekday buttons, or pick a one-off date from the calendar.
- See today and future dates in Scheduled Dates. Past entries stay saved.

## Timers and overlays

- When you defer a break, the cursor notice briefly shows the new countdown. The HUD, menu bar, Now popover, and Glance keep counting down to the rescheduled break.
- Glance and Break Screen can stay visible when you switch macOS Spaces, including eligible full-screen Spaces.
- Timer boundaries and pre-break warnings play sound when enabled in Crona. The daemon can try its fallback if local playback fails.

## Daemon compatibility

This beta requires a Crona daemon compatible with protocol **1.7**, unchanged from beta.7. Starting a fresh focus segment when you extend a Pomodoro after its final break requires an updated daemon that includes that timer fix.
