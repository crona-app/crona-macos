# Crona for macOS v1.1.0-beta.4

This beta adds a Library window for check-in history and longer-term Review, a compact daily Stats summary, and richer issue and habit details with actions that stay visible.

## Library: check-in history and Review

- Browse and search recorded check-ins, read full notes, edit an existing record without changing its date, or delete it after confirmation. Creation and update timestamps use the daemon's timezone and your date preference.
- Review calendar weeks and months with work, focus, and wellbeing charts; plan outcomes; estimate accuracy; and comparisons with the preceding period.
- Hover over a graph for a nearby tooltip, date guide, and highlighted points. Recorded away days are marked in the charts.
- Open History from Wellbeing or Review from Stats using the shortcut beside the date. Both open the same resizable Library window, which stays separate from the popover's selected day and does not enter fullscreen.

## A compact daily summary

Stats now brings Score, Worked, Issues, and Habits together in four gauges. Focus percentage sits with the score, while sessions, average session length, and breaks share a single row below. Targets, failed habits, abandoned issues, and available high-risk counts remain visible.

Recent issue changes appear directly in an activity timeline with timestamps, event icons, readable status changes, and links to available issues.

## Richer details, reachable actions

- Issue details show a time-used ring, worked time, and the remaining estimate or overrun. Tracked time represents effort, not completion.
- Habit details show 7- and 30-day hit-rate rings, a two-week outcome strip, and recent entries. Hit rates use recorded outcomes; missing days are excluded.
- Previous/next controls and arrow keys browse the current filtered list. Returning to the list preserves its position.
- Issue and habit actions stay in a fixed footer while details scroll. The habit duration editor stays there too.

## Consistent historical away days

Issues, Habits, Wellbeing, and Stats show one recovery panel on a recorded away date. Daily content and creation controls are hidden, while date navigation, the calendar, History, and Review remain available. Returning to a normal day restores its content.

## Reliability fixes

- Recover cleanly after daemon disconnects without an older connection attempt replacing a newer one.
- Clear stale issue lists when switching dates and ignore late responses for previously selected days.
- Keep newer tag-filter selections when saves overlap or settings refresh during a save.
- Invalidate calendar summaries after check-in changes without moving the popover's selected date.

Requires a Crona daemon compatible with protocol **1.7**, unchanged from the previous beta.
