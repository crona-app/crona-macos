# Crona for macOS v1.1.0-beta.3

The third 1.1.0 beta follows Crona's move to the `crona-app` GitHub organization. Updates now come from crona.work, the focus-complete popups are calmer, and an optional celebration marks a finished focus block.

## Updates move to crona.work

The app now checks `https://crona.work/updates/mac/appcast.xml` for updates. That address follows the update feed wherever it is hosted, so future moves will not interrupt updates. Links to docs, GitHub, Support, and Feedback & Roadmap now open the `crona-app` organization, and feedback goes to GitHub Discussions instead of UserJot.

## Easier-to-notice updates

- When an update is available, the menu bar icon shows an up-arrow inside the C and a larger dot in its corner. During a session, the dot sits beside the progress ring.
- The popup shows a small **Crona X available** pill pinned to its bottom; click it to update.

## A calmer focus-complete popup

- **Focus block complete** shows a ring that settles into a checkmark, the time you focused, and which focus session of the day it was.
- **Add more time** offers **+1 min**, **+5 min**, and **+15 min** chips (or sessions), still on keys 1 to 3.
- **End Session** asks **What did you get done?** with the focused time.
- All three keep one issue card with the title, repo and stream, and time against the estimate.

## Completion Celebration

Settings > Notifications > Focus Boundaries > **Completion Celebration** adds an optional flourish when a focus block completes:

- **None**: the checkmark ring only (default).
- **Glow**: a soft green glow along the edges of the active screen.
- **Confetti**: two confetti cannons from the bottom corners of the active screen, aimed at the popup.

Both play behind the popup and never take clicks or keys. Reduce Motion turns them off. Choosing an option or pressing **Preview** plays it.

## Fixes

- The Issues tab follows the daemon's day after midnight and after the daemon reconnects, instead of showing yesterday until the app restarts.

## Compatibility

- Release tag: `v1.1.0-beta.3`.
- Apple marketing version: `1.1.0`; build: `21`.
- Semantic release version: `1.1.0-beta.3`; channel: `beta`.
- Requires a Crona daemon reporting protocol `1.7`, the same as beta 2.
